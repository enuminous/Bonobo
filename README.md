**Behavioral Observation of Negotiation, Organization, Bonding & Optimization**

BONOBO is an experimental EFMW-inspired framework for evaluating cooperative intelligence, negotiation quality, coalition behavior, conflict resolution, and mutually beneficial optimization across multiple actors.

## Core question

> Can a group improve collective outcomes without hiding exploitation, coercion, or unstable bargains inside apparent cooperation?

BONOBO focuses on the quality and durability of cooperation.

## Primary outputs

- negotiation efficiency
- mutual-gain score
- fairness dispersion
- coalition stability
- conflict-resolution quality
- trust-update coherence
- exploitative-gain penalty
- cooperative optimization score
