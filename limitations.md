# Limitations and Falsification

BONOBO is an experimental cooperation framework.

## Limitations

- Utilities may be difficult to measure.
- Fairness is partly normative.
- Actors may prefer unequal but acceptable outcomes.
- Trust proxies can be crude.
- Coalition stability does not imply fairness.
- Conflict resolution can be domain-specific.

## Falsification criteria

BONOBO's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Standard bargaining metrics perform equivalently.
2. Trust updates add no predictive value.
3. Exploitation penalties do not improve long-run stability prediction.
4. Fairness dispersion does not correlate with coalition durability.
5. Results fail across held-out negotiation structures.
6. Cooperative optimization scores do not predict better repeated outcomes.

## Claim boundary

BONOBO evaluates observable cooperative structure under supplied utility models. It does not infer private motives, sincerity, or moral character.
